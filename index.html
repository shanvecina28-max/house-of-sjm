Behind House of SJM

Our Story

Founded in 2026 in Sauyo, Quezon City, House of SJM was born out of a passion for high-quality, ultra-crispy Chicharon Bagnet. Through strict quality standards, trusted local sourcing, and meticulous selection, House of SJM ensures that only premium-grade Chicharon Bagnet reaches your table.

The Founders & Operators

Christian V. Crisanto and Wife

As a hands-on, husband-and-wife-run business, Christian and his wife personally manage, oversee, and operate House of SJM together. They ensure that every single batch is carefully inspected, packed, and prepared with utmost care and dedication.
